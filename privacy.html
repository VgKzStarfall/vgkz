VVM Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

1. Who we are and how to contact us

VVM (Việt Vệ Minh) is a video game developed by VGKZ, an independent game developer. This policy describes the personal data that VVM processes when you play it.

Contact and data-protection contact: vgkzpenman@gmail.com.

2. What data we collect and how

VGKZ does not run servers that receive or store player data. VVM has no analytics, no advertising identifiers, no crash-report upload, no payment data, no location data, and no contact data.

Sign-in with Epic Games

Online play uses Epic Online Services (EOS) and an Epic Games account. When you sign in, VVM requests three permissions: Basic Profile, Friends List, and Online Presence. Through them, the game receives:

  • your Epic account ID and display name (Basic Profile);
  • your friends list (Friends List);
  • your presence, meaning your online and in-game status (Online Presence).

The game does not receive your Epic Games password.

Data stored on your PC

The game stores the following on your PC, in the game's Saved folder and in the local sign-in cache:

  • settings, key bindings, and graphics options;
  • loadout, progression, and cosmetic selections;
  • log files with session events and connection state. A log file may contain your display name and the IP address of the host;
  • a sign-in token, so later launches sign you in automatically.

Multiplayer session data

During a multiplayer session, the host player's PC receives the display name, account ID, in-game actions, and network address (IP address or EOS peer ID) of the other players. Other players in the session see your display name and your in-game actions.

Session details, meaning the room name, map, player count, and host display name, are published through Epic Online Services so that players can find and join the room.

3. How we use your data

We use the data only to:

  • sign you in;
  • run multiplayer sessions;
  • show your friends and your online status, show theirs to you, and send or accept session invites. The friends list is used only for this purpose;
  • keep your settings, loadout, and progress;
  • diagnose connection problems from log files that you choose to send us.

4. Who we share your data with

  • Other players in your session. They see your display name and your in-game actions. The host player's PC also receives your account ID and network address, as described in section 2.
  • Epic Online Services. VVM uses EOS for sign-in, friends, presence, and session matchmaking. Session details are published through EOS, as described in section 2.

VGKZ does not sell or share your data with anyone else.

Epic Games' handling of your Epic Games account is described in the Epic Games Privacy Policy.

5. How long we keep your data

  • Data stored on your PC stays there until you delete it or uninstall VVM.
  • Session details exist only while the session is open.
  • Log files that you email to us are kept for at most 90 days, and then deleted. We hold no other copy.

6. How to access or delete your data

  • Data on your PC. You can open the game's Saved folder to see the local data. To delete it, delete the Saved folder. You can also uninstall VVM.
  • Epic Games access. You can revoke VVM's access to your Epic account at any time. Open your Epic Games account settings and go to connected apps.
  • Requests to us. Email vgkzpenman@gmail.com to ask what personal data we hold about you or to request its deletion. We answer within 30 days.

7. Data we do not collect

VVM does not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, crash reports, payment data, location data, or contacts. VGKZ does not receive or store player data on any server.

8. Children

VVM is not directed at children under 13.

9. Changes to this policy

We update this policy when the data that VVM processes changes. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated.
