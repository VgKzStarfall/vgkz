About the game

VVM (Việt Vệ Minh) is a video game made by VGKZ. It is a cooperative tactical squad shooter, played in first person on Windows PC and built in Unreal Engine 5. A small squad of players takes contracts in large natural valley, forest and river maps with dynamic weather and wind.

VVM is in development. It is in closed playtests with invited players and is not sold yet.

Features

  • Co-op squads. Play with your squad in a host-based co-op session. One player hosts the session.
  • Bounty contracts. Short runs on a single map.
  • Expedition contracts. Runs across several maps. New players can join mid-run.
  • Large natural maps. Valley, forest and river maps with dynamic weather and wind.
  • Operator classes and loadouts. Choose an operator class and a loadout.
  • Tactical combat. Fight AI-controlled hostile squads, gather loot and extract.
