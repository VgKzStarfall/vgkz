VVM

Cooperative squad survival shooter

About the product

VVM is a first-person squad survival shooter developed by VGKZ. A small squad takes a hostile valley on a contract, holds ground, and extracts with what it carried in. Runs are limited: bounty contracts are short and closed to late arrivals, expedition contracts span several maps and accept new players mid-run.

VVM is a Windows build, distributed through the VGKZ launcher. It is in active development, and playtests run through invited builds.

Organization

VGKZ is an independent game development organization. VVM is its current product. Contact: vgkzpenman@gmail.com.

Features

  • Squad cooperative play over Epic Online Services.
  • Bounty contracts: short, single-map, closed once the run starts.
  • Expedition contracts: multi-map runs that accept new players mid-run.
  • Procedural valley, forest, and river maps with weather and wind.
  • Squad AI combatants, class loadouts, and loot extraction.

Privacy

Multiplayer sessions use Epic Online Services, which signs players in with an Epic Games account. Read the privacy policy for what VVM stores and what Epic Games processes on our behalf.
